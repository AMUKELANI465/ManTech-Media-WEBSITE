Privacy Policy.

How Mantech Media handles your data, and an honest note on our editorial accuracy.

What we collect

We do not collect email addresses or any personal information. When you click "Subscribe" on this site, we save a small note in your own browser's storage (localStorage) so we can show you which stories are new since your last visit. That note never leaves your device and we have no access to it.

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising on this site.

Our reporting

Stories on Mantech Media are written and edited by our team. We do our best to check facts before publishing, but we are a small publication and mistakes can happen. Please treat our articles as a starting point, not the final word — especially for anything involving prices, availability, health, legal or financial decisions. We'd encourage you to verify important details independently before acting on them.

If you spot something inaccurate, please get in touch and we'll correct it.
